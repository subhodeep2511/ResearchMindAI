# Project Brief

## Title
ResearchMind AI: An Autonomous Research Analysis, Knowledge Discovery and Research Gap Identification Platform

## Problem statement
Researchers often review papers manually across disconnected PDFs. Comparing methods, datasets, findings and limitations across a collection is time-consuming, while generic summarization tools may produce claims that are difficult to trace to source evidence.

## Proposed solution
ResearchMind AI provides a local research workspace that ingests academic PDFs, extracts structured information, retrieves evidence passages, compares studies, surfaces corpus-relative candidate research directions and exports a review report.

## Objectives
1. Build a searchable academic paper library.
2. Extract text and basic metadata from PDF publications.
3. Retrieve relevant evidence for research questions.
4. Compare multiple studies using common analytical dimensions.
5. Identify recurring limitations and underrepresented concepts.
6. Visualize paper-concept relationships.
7. Generate an editable literature review report.
8. Evaluate retrieval and extraction quality against human-reviewed annotations.

## Scope
The initial release supports text-based PDFs, local SQLite storage, TF-IDF retrieval and heuristic research analysis. OCR, neural embeddings, external scholarly metadata, citation graph ingestion and LLM-based synthesis are future extensions.

## Novelty positioning
The project is an integrated, evidence-traceable research workflow rather than a general-purpose PDF chatbot. Its academic contribution should be demonstrated through evaluation of retrieval quality, extraction quality and the usefulness of candidate research directions on a curated corpus. Do not claim that the system independently proves novelty.

## Expected outcome
A working local web application, a reproducible test suite, a documented architecture, a curated evaluation dataset and an experimental report.
